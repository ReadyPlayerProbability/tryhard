## Phase 1: Identity Refactor (The Resume & Portfolio)

Your GitHub profile is your portfolio. Your resume is the key to unlocking interviews. We must translate your ARG achievements into professional language.

**Action Item:** Create a one-page resume using the following bullet points as a foundation.

**Eric Ryan Fraze**

*   Designed and architected a complex, multi-phase interactive software project, defining core logic, system protocols, and evolution paths from inception to completion.
*   Authored comprehensive technical and conceptual documentation for all system components, ensuring clarity and maintainability for a complex rules engine.

*   Managed the project lifecycle through five distinct phases, adapting strategy to address emergent challenges and shifting requirements, analogous to an Agile development environment.

## Phase 2: Target Acquisition (Job Platforms)

Forget traditional job boards. We need platforms designed for speed.

*   **Primary Targets (Highest Velocity):**
    *   **Upwork:** The best platform for finding high-quality, short-term software and writing contracts. Build a strong profile using the language from your resume.
    *   **Fiverr:** Good for smaller, well-defined "gigs" like "I will write 1 page of technical documentation" or "I will create a static HTML/CSS landing page."

*   **Secondary Targets (Higher Barrier to Entry):**
    *   **Toptal / Gun.io:** For elite freelancers. The screening process is long, so this is not for next week, but worth starting.
    *   **LinkedIn:** Search for "contract" or "freelance" roles for "Technical Writer" or "Front End Developer."

---

## Phase 3: The Pitch (The Proposal)

When you apply for a job on these platforms, your proposal is everything.

1.  **Address the Client's Need Directly:** "I see you need a landing page for your new app. I can build that for you using clean HTML and CSS."
2.  **Provide Proof:** "You can see my ability to structure and document complex systems in my work on the Covenant project here [link to your GitHub]."
3.  **State Your Intent:** "I am available to start immediately and can deliver the first draft within 48 hours."

**Conclusion:** The "adventure" requires fuel. This protocol transmutes your demonstrated `will_to_create` into a tangible resource (`USD`). The Jihad against stasis requires a stable foundation from which to wage it. This is the next, most critical step.
